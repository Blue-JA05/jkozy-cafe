House Rules

Keep it cozy for everyone. These are the same rules posted in café-rules.

  1. 🤬

    1. Don't Be a Jerk

    Treat everyone with basic respect. Friendly banter is fine. Harassment, bullying, and targeted negativity are not.

  2. 🌼

    2. No Hate Speech

    No racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, or discrimination of any kind.

  3. 😳

    3. Keep It SFW

    No NSFW content, explicit media, or inappropriate discussions. Keep the server comfortable for everyone.

  4. 🧑‍💻

    4. No Spam

    Don't flood chats with repeated messages, emoji walls, excessive caps, or constant pings.

  5. ☑️

    5. Use the Right Channels

    Keep conversations in the channels they belong in. It helps everyone find what they're looking for.

  6. 📬

    6. No Advertising

    Don't promote your server, socials, streams, products, or services without permission from staff.

  7. 🪪

    7. Respect Privacy

    Don't share personal information about yourself or others. No doxxing, stalking, or digging into people's private lives.

  8. 🕵️

    8. No Impersonation

    Don't pretend to be another member, moderator, creator, or public figure.

  9. 📜

    9. Follow Discord ToS

    If Discord says it's not allowed, it's not allowed here either.

  10. 🧑‍⚖️

    10. Listen to Staff

    Moderators are here to keep things running smoothly. If you have an issue, discuss it respectfully in private.

  11. 😤

    11. Leave the Drama at the Door

    Personal arguments and outside-server drama should stay outside the server.

  12. 🤦

    12. No Backseating Unless Asked

    Whether it's gaming, art, content creation, or tech support, don't force advice on others unless they ask for it.

  13. 😵‍💫

    13. Don't Spam Ping the Creator

    Please don't repeatedly ping the streamer/Vtuber or staff for attention. They'll respond when they're available.

  14. 🎨

    14. Credit Artists and Creators

    If you share artwork (no AI artwork allowed), models, music, or other creative content, give proper credit whenever possible.

  15. 🛡️

    15. Respect Boundaries

    If someone says they're uncomfortable with a topic, joke, or interaction, respect that and move on.

If a rule gets broken

  1. ⚠️1st timeWarning
  2. ⏳2nd timeTemporary ban
  3. 🚫3rd timePermanent ban

Members without the 🌱 Verified role get checked every weekend, so don't forget to click the green check!!

Partners & FAQ

Got a question?? It's probably here.

How do I partner with JKozy Café??

DM JustJA or open a ticket in tickety and tell us about your server or your content. If it's a good fit, you'll show up in partner-servers or partner-creators, and creators get the 🤝 Partnered Creators role.

How do I become a mod??

Head to mod-application in the Help Desk and fill out an application saying why you want to join the JKozy Café team. Staff will look it over and get back to you.

I need help or want to report something. What do I do??

Open a private ticket with Tickety in the tickety channel. Only you and staff can see it.

Where do I ask a general question??

Make a post in questions with a title that sums up what you're asking so people can find it later.

Why can't I see all the channels??

You need the 🌱 Verified role first. Go to café-rules, read them, and click the green check mark at the bottom.

Is the server SFW??

Yes!! It's strictly SFW. Anything NSFW gets you a warning or worse.

Can I share AI art??

Nope. No AI artwork is allowed, and you should credit artists and creators whenever you share their stuff.
